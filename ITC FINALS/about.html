Our story

A cafe built by students, for students

Campus Cafe began as a simple idea: every campus needs a warm place that feels like a break, not another classroom. We are a small team who love coffee, good music and the sound of friends laughing between lectures.

Today we serve hundreds of cups a week and host study nights, open mics and quiet corners for anyone who needs them.

What we care about

Welcome for everyone

Every student, teacher and visitor gets the same friendly greeting and the same fair price.

Fresh and local

We choose ingredients from nearby suppliers whenever we can, so your food is fresh and the community benefits.

Calm by design

Soft lighting, gentle music and comfortable seats make it easy to focus or simply breathe for a while.

Listen to our cafe playlist

Press play to hear the music we use to set the mood during morning study hours.
